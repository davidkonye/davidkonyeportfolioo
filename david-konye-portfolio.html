Who I Am

Passion.
Process.
Purpose.

Throughout my career, I have always tried to ensure that the passion and enthusiasm for my work shines through. Despite my success as an independent designer, I have found that it is through a team that I have achieved my greatest potential. I have learned that having a platform to question and develop ideas harnesses greater creativity — although realizing creative inspiration doesn't come without its obstacles. Time management and working to tight deadlines are important in any industry, but the foundations and biggest challenges of design lie in communication.
